A MORE MINDFUL WAY TO OPEN APPS

Digital wellbeing

A balanced pause between the impulse and the app.

Early concept
◌

Make room for a choice.

Choose an app, set a short waiting period, and decide whether opening it still feels right. When time is up, you can continue or head back to what you were doing.

Balanced by design

A gentle delay you control. No shame, streak penalties, or locked-in restrictions.

Emergency bypass

Always keep a clear route to open an app immediately when you need it.

This is a design exploration only. Android app restrictions require platform permissions and careful implementation; this web preview does not control apps on your device.
